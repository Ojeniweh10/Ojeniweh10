I build, integrate, fix, and maintain production backend systems for early-stage SaaS teams — so founders and CTOs get engineering capacity **without** the recruiting cycle, salary overhead, and long-term commitment of another full-time hire.

If your team has:
- an MVP already live with real users
- backend work piling up faster than you can ship it

- a lean engineering team that needs extra hands, not another headcount
